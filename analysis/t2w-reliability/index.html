    Preprocessing¶

    Smriprep does not directly output the individual T2w image that have been corrected for intensity non-uniformity (INU). To get access to the bias-field corrected individual T2w, we copied them from smriprep work directory into inu_corrected_individual_T2w. Specifially, they were stored in $WORKDIR/smriprep_wf/single_subject_001_wf/anat_preproc_wf/anat_fit_wf/t2w_template_wf/n4_correct/mapflow/_n4_correct*/*.nii.gz. Let's first load those INU-corrected T2w images.

    !! Careful when interpreting the coefficient of variation !!¶

    Our T2-weighted sequence was not quantitative, so we cannot assume that the T2-weighted signal intensity in each voxel remains consistent across sessions. Additionally, the segmentation is not as clean as in the case of the T1w image. To improve the segmentation there are two possibilities:

    • Re-run FSL fast on T1w and T2w images simultaneously
    • Retrieve in the smriprep work directory to transformation from T2w to T1w space, align all T2w images to the T1w space and re-use the segmentation computed by smriprep on T1w images. The path to the transformation in the smriprep workdirectory is anat_preproc_wf/anat_fit_wf/t2wtot1w_xfm
